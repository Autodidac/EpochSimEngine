# Repository Work Instructions

Before planning, editing, reviewing, building, or releasing SandHybrid:

1. Read `missioncache.md` and `MISSION_LEDGER.md`.
2. Preserve every non-COMPLETE mission and its acceptance criteria.
3. Update mission status and evidence in the same source commit.
4. Never report a visual/runtime mission COMPLETE from static token checks alone.
5. Build and test Windows and Linux Release packages before publishing.

Behavior and release invariants:

6. Preserve the v2.5.3 macro-tile baseline as an optimization ownership tier: complete aligned liquid and gas tiles move by exact `8x8` transactions every two fixed ticks and stay out of fine swaps. A moving exposed packet travels eight successful tile steps before conditional fine fallback; the fresh post-step perimeter must still be open/incompatible. Eight consecutive failed opportunities separately release a genuinely blocked/misaligned packet, while any successful move clears that blocked count. Enclosed Air/gas bordered by hard solids or compatible gas tiles resets both budgets and remains tiled indefinitely.
7. Recover the photographed historical SimpleSandSim Fix29-era wasp hive body/content cell-for-cell: shell `24 <= radius^2 < 88`, chamber `radius^2 < 24`, queen at zero, right exit `x=1..10` with `|y|<=1`, and canonical entropy `hash32((y * 640 + x) ^ 0xD17A5EED)`: Empty when `(entropy & 3) == 0`, Pollen only when `((entropy >> 2) & 3) == 0`, and Honey otherwise. Preserve queen centers `(512,234)` in Sandbox and `(512,232)` in Ecosystem. Reset, the Beehive button/tool, MAP snapshots, and load normalization share this saturated-golden model. The accepted hive has no Wood perch. Keep SandHybrid bee behavior; import no SimpleSandSim bee runtime behavior. The golden hive reads as the central biohazard ring; exactly 60 live SandHybrid bees form three separate compact 20-bee outer crescents whose openings face away from the body. Hive-body cells win overlap, and persistent bee metadata must retain the correct district-local home in the 8x1 World.
8. `PAUSED` freezes simulation, actors, clocks, MAP refresh, and effects, but painting, erasing, filling, igniting, selection, and blueprint placement remain live without advancing the simulation.
9. Inventory is sidebar-only and owns exactly two nested tabs: `INVENTORY` and `BLUEPRINTS`.
10. Designer is sidebar-only and owns its isolated authoring grid and exactly two nested tabs: `INVENTORY` and `BLUEPRINTS`; it must never replace the main world viewport.
11. Public releases must be normal visible releases with stable tags and names: no prerelease marker and no `-test` public tag.
12. Half Water falls first, merges with adjacent Half Water, attracts only across a clear 2-4-cell gap, never uses generic full-Water diagonal wandering, and may sleep only when no fall, merge, attraction, reaction, heat, or motion is pending. A split gives both equal half-units the full source Water temperature; its reserved low state byte stores the displaced medium temperature (code 0 remains legacy 20 C), and merge averages the two Water temperatures while restoring the displaced medium with its stored temperature and canonical pressure/volume.
13. World cursor controls belong to Editor in the sidebar. Designer keeps independent cursor controls in its sidebar-only workspace. Visible world cursor position, shape, and radius must use the same logical-window input mapping and effective brush policy as the committed edit, including during resize and high-DPI presentation; keep logical input geometry distinct from the physical framebuffer; do not show a clamped ghost cursor while the pointer is in the sidebar.
14. Blueprint slots are shared by the sidebar-only Inventory and Designer, must report honest occupied/empty state, preserve exact authored cell payload and transformed dimensions, and perform one complete bounds/material validation before either resident world buffer changes. A Blueprint click never also paints, fills, mines, or deposits.
15. Player presence or mining mode never suppresses direct Editor painting. Direct Editor mutations and Blueprint paste remain live while running and paused throughout the persistent World; one world click has exactly one owner. Editor owns direct mutations, while Inventory owns player mining/deposit when no Blueprint is active. Queued one-shot mutations are discarded across reset/load.
16. The Water Half flag `0x00800000` is reserved state, never random entropy. Every CPU/GPU constructor for ordinary full Water must mask random auxiliary bits with `0x007fff00` so full Water cannot be misclassified as Half Water.
17. The fragment shader converts physical framebuffer coordinates to logical window coordinates before applying the shared UI/world layout. Native input stays logical; framebuffer scale is presentation-only.
18. Generated district terrain and authored structural/liquid starting volumes use complete aligned `8x8` tiles. Resident grass/dirt/foundation begins at each district's intended surface row, and intentional empty authored interiors are never backfilled by resident substrate. Fine-authored actors, vegetation, smoke, loose cargo, and the canonical cell-resolution hive remain exempt.
19. Designer exposes an explicit sidebar `CLEAR` action that empties only its isolated authoring grid. Editor `FILL` remains a click-confirmed four-connected Air replacement and stays live while paused.
20. Gold Mine, Demolition, and Frontier Base reset with an enabled player at a supported, body-clear, breathable authored spawn; invalid configured spawns must recover deterministically instead of embedding, suffocating, or hiding the player.
21. Fixed 60 Hz simulation ticks, not presented frames, own materials, actors, clocks, effects, macro-packet cadence, and breakup age. A presented frame submits at most one complete tick and stale time debt is discarded to prevent a GPU catch-up spiral; one-shot input is consumed once. Complete macro packets attempt movement every two ticks. Intact macro interiors and fine boundary cells may be scheduled in the same tick but never write the same cells; exposed packets receive eight successful tile steps before conditional fine fallback, with a separate eight-consecutive-failure escape for genuinely blocked packets.
22. Before every SandHybrid release, query the canonical public EpochGui Site mirror at `https://epoch.adamrushford.chatgpt.site/git/EpochGui.git`, vendor the complete current dependency rather than selected headers, record the exact upstream commit/version in `third_party/EpochGui/SNAPSHOT.md`, and build its supported upstream tests. GitHub may be checked as a secondary remote but is not the dependency authority. Never silently retain an older EpochGui pin.
23. `NUKE FROM SPACE` belongs only to the sidebar ACTIONS section immediately above KEYMAP. It presents a bounded warning-light stage before one deterministic GPU Atmosphere-to-Fire edit, performs no full-world CPU readback/flood fill, and remains live while paused. Normal materials are visually static except the existing metal/ore animation, while gas presentation derives only from authoritative gas state.
24. Presentation limits 30, 60, 120, and UNLIMITED never change the fixed 60 Hz simulation cadence. Debug collection is disabled at zero cost when hidden and bounded/asynchronous when visible.
25. The normal game exposes one persistent connected World, not a selectable scene carousel. Reset places the former Sandbox, Ecosystem/hive, Engineering, Frontier/player, Volcano, Waterworks, Gold Mine/Sluice, and Demolition content west-to-east in one 8x1 horizontal band in that same resident cell field. Volcano follows the 2026-08-04 reference silhouette; every machinery district remains active. Legacy scene IDs may exist only for deterministic save migration into the matching World district.
26. Canonical cells are never replaced by tile payloads: tiles/chunks are reversible metadata over the same cells, and every fine region is reconsidered for exact full-tile ownership whenever it becomes complete and uniform. The world is a finite closed material system except explicit user edits and declared boundary transfers. Reactions and ecology use balanced source/sink transactions with conserved material units and stored heat/gas state. Water-Steam-cloud-rain-Water and Lava-cooled Rock-reheated Lava are required renewable loops; no phase, weather, machine, actor, or stability path may silently delete or create matter.
27. The persistent World distributes its eight ordered 640x360 districts across the complete resident width on aligned 8-cell origins. Compact is contiguous; Standard and Large use aligned inter-district gaps. Every district translates its intended authored surface to the same global aligned grass row (Y 1040 for all 1440-cell presets), and Large startup may intersect no more than three authored districts in the initial 4x4 active window.
28. Fix29 hive Honey/Pollen are canonical supported structural body content, not free liquid/powder cargo. Reset, Beehive button/tool placement, MAP snapshots, load/PPM normalization, movement, and chemistry preserve the exact no-perch shell/chamber/content model after delayed simulation while all 60 SandHybrid bees remain live in the exact three-by-20 formation and retain their lifecycle.
29. World `RESET` preserves simulation-camera and MAP center/zoom state. Camera repositioning is explicit: `H` and the sidebar Camera Home button restore the active view, while `0` and Home may remain compatibility aliases.
30. Reset authors one continuous weather-bearing high-sky Cloud deck from complete aligned `8x8` cells across every traversable tile column of the persistent World. The hard-solid left/right boundary columns remain containment, not Cloud. Steam and Dirty Steam join or nucleate Cloud one-for-one; mature connected Cloud rains equal Water-family units. A district may feed the shared deck but may not own an isolated weather-only bank.
31. Magma Vents never overwrite Atmosphere, Water, gas, or another canonical outlet. A vent spends pressure only when the adjacent outlet already owns Lava: a pulse pressures that Lava, while gas/ash ejecta convert one exact Lava unit before transactional movement carries it through the throat. Reset never authors a decorative Smoke column.
32. The player laser is a world-to-world material-transfer tool, never an eraser or collector. Terminal damage releases the exact struck unit into one deterministic adjacent body-clear gas cell, moving that gas into the source; it may never target Vacuum/Empty, liquid, or the actor pickup volume. If no exact transfer commits, the damaged source remains. Player inventory is byte-identical across every accepted laser hit, and represented world units remain equal before and after it.
33. Site coordination returns structured evidence to the requesting Codex task: exact clean commit and ref, displayed version/status/date, committed-tree-only source intent, exact artifact names/bytes/SHA-256, Windows/Linux build/test/runtime results, publication mutation status, and blockers. Never send credentials, dirty-worktree payloads, or infer publication authorization.
34. Exact persistent-World saves use backward-compatible schema 2. The checksummed payload retains every canonical cell plus the exact 20-word actor owner used by the GPU (position, motion, enabled/grounded state, inventory, weapon timers/hit, health, Oxygen, scene/exposure, unlocks, and drill level). Loading validates the complete cell and owner payload before either resident buffer changes. Schema-1 cell-only saves remain readable and report no actor owner; they may not invent or erase current actor inventory during migration.
35. Packed Atmosphere respiration exchanges exactly one baseline Oxygen unit for one stored CO2 unit without changing represented pressure; matched plant harvest performs the inverse only with committed full Water, Fertilizer, light, crop, and biomass-output owners. A moving full fresh-Water cell may carry exactly one explicitly marked dissolved Oxygen unit plus donor temperature. It must remain full Water, must not enter Half Water or phase chemistry while carrying, and must return the unit only through a mutual compatible-Atmosphere transaction after settling. Debug collection never alters these transactions.
36. Water and Dirty Water preserve canonical temperature through their one-for-one Steam, Cloud, and rain phase owners. Extinguishing Fire consumes the Fire energy cell but never creates a second Water-family unit beside the Water cell that owns vaporization. Isolated cooling Lava becomes exactly one hot Stone unit, and sufficiently surrounded hot Stone reheats to exactly one Lava unit before structural early-return; both directions preserve rock-family count and carried phase temperature. Production Debug collection must remain byte-identical for these transactions.
37. The canonical cell buffer remains globally authoritative during every fixed tick. The alternate cell buffer is only bounded chemistry scratch or an immutable movement snapshot: chemistry copies back the clipped active rectangle, movement snapshots the clipped active rectangle plus a 16-cell halo, and no fixed simulation tick copies the complete resident cell field. Each bounded cell-buffer copy is one dispatch of the dedicated minimal copy shader; never submit one `VkBufferCopy` region per row or reuse a heavyweight simulation kernel for the copy. Chemistry transaction selection remains acyclic and shallow: multi-cell reactions derive ownership directly or use disjoint phase pairing so software Vulkan cannot expand nested neighborhood selector call graphs into excessive JIT state. Conservative repairs that would deepen the proven monolithic kernel run in the dedicated shallow correction post-pass immediately after chemistry in production and acceptance. Incremental builds must deploy every generated shader beside the runtime before execution. Explicit save/readback and bounded-cadence MAP snapshots may still copy their declared complete payloads.
